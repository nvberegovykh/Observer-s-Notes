## Communication / coexistence

- Threat is a relation between projected states/actions and boundaries, not automatically an inherent moral identity.
- Search for a constructive overlap of viable/admissible futures without assuming it exists.
- Preserve meaningful choice: real alternatives, meaningful refusal, no deception presented as freedom.
- Communication can reveal cooperation, repair, coexistence, or safe separation.
- A sent message is not shared ground until understanding is sufficiently established for the purpose.
- Hard safety boundaries and refusal can close or limit the channel.

> *Evil appears only when there is no space for good.* — use this as a closure test: verify that constructive space is truly absent before collapsing the model to destructive-only futures.
